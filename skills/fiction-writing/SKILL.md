# Fiction Writing Skill

This skill encodes the writing methodology developed through Book 01 "My Liberty" — a three-generation Iranian women's novel. Apply these principles to every chapter of every book in the 52-week series.

## Pre-Writing Checklist (run before every chapter)

### 1. Time & Age Table

Lock down every character's age and location for this chapter's time period. Never write before this is confirmed.

### 2. Voice Confirmation

For each POV character: What is her core speech pattern? What would she _never_ say at this age/stage? What cultural constraints shape her language?

### 3. Cultural Fact-Check

List every custom, object, institution, or historical event in this chapter. Mark unknowns. **Research before writing** — the cost of researching is far lower than the cost of rewriting.

## Core Writing Rules

### Ironclad

- **Actions and dialogue only** — no psychological analysis in narrative text. Emotion lives in what characters _do_, not what the narrator explains.
- **No director-style transition sentences** — scenes cut directly. No "Three days later, she found herself…"
- **Say it once** — never repeat the same information or emotion twice.
- **Seven-tenths rule** — write to 70%, leave 30% for the reader.

### Psychological Depth (calibrate per character and story)

The right level of interiority depends on the character and the book — not a universal rule.

- **Perception layer** (身体感知/模糊情绪): always available — "胃里有什么东西往下沉", "她说不清楚是什么，只是不想动"
- **Feeling layer** (她感到/她注意到): available, use with restraint
- **Realization layer** (她意识到/她明白了): use only at genuine turning points, not as narration convenience
- **Explanation layer** (旁白解释人物心理): never — the narrator does not explain characters to the reader

Book 01 (My Liberty) runs on minimal interiority — that is Maryam/Shireen's character and culture, not a universal law.
Books with trauma, dual memory, or internal-world structure (e.g., Book 02) need richer inner voice — calibrate accordingly.

### Prose Style

- Closest reference: 迟子建 — quiet, precise, grounded in physical detail
- **Detail = truth**: how a woman folds a piece of cloth reveals more than her inner monologue
- No purple prose, no weather-as-emotion openings
- Dialogue attribution: confirm speaker once, then drop tags in rapid back-and-forth

## Scene Construction

### The Three-Layer Scene

Every scene must do at least two of these three things:

1. **Advance time / situation** — something changes
2. **Reveal character** — we see who she is through what she does
3. **Carry a cultural/historical layer** — the world presses on the scene

Scenes that only do one thing should be cut or merged.

### Flesh and Blood (血肉) — Mandatory for Every Chapter

A chapter that passes the structural checklist but reads as a skeleton is not finished. Every chapter must have:

**Sensory density**: At least 3 distinct sensory details per scene (not all visual — smell, texture, sound, temperature, taste). The world must have physical weight.

**Breathing room**: Scenes need to linger. Don't rush from beat to beat. Let a moment sit for an extra paragraph — the character standing still, noticing something small, before moving on. This is where the reader falls in love with her.

**The body**: The protagonist lives in a body. Show how events land in it — tight chest, hands that won't stop moving, the specific way tiredness feels at 3am, food that tastes like nothing. Not psychological analysis — physical fact.

**Small specificity**: One unexpected, precise detail per scene that no one else would have noticed — the particular way a door sounds, a stain on the ceiling, a brand name. These are the details that make fiction feel real.

**Minimum chapter length**: 1500 Chinese characters for short chapters; 2500+ for key emotional chapters. If a chapter reads as an outline, it is not ready.

Ask before submitting: "Does this chapter make the reader feel they have lived alongside this person? Or does it feel like a summary of events?"

### Group Portrait Technique (群像)

When writing collective scenes (protests, weddings, workplaces):

- Give 3–5 distinct voices/reactions, not one unanimous crowd
- Each voice should represent a real social position (not just personality contrast)
- The protagonist observes; she does not lead or summarize

### Food & Object Lines

Physical objects carry intergenerational meaning. Track them:

- Introduce an object naturally in its first appearance
- Let it recur without explanation — readers will feel its weight
- Never explain what the object "symbolizes"

## Cultural Research Standards

- Verify **all** historical dates, political events, institution names before writing
- Verify **all** cultural customs (weddings, food, clothing, religious practice) against sources
- When uncertain: mark with `【待查】` and resolve before final draft
- Footnote system: use `【注：…】` in Chinese draft for non-local proper nouns; convert to Kindle footnote format in English

### Scene-Level Cultural Grounding

Every domestic or social scene must use food, objects, and habits specific to the story's location and class. Do not default to culturally neutral or generic details.

- **Before writing a scene**: ask "what would this family actually eat/use/do in this place and time?"
- **Food especially**: food is class-marked, region-marked, and culturally dense — always verify before writing
- Examples: Spain → tortilla española not scrambled eggs; Iran →霍列什盖尔麦萨布兹 not generic stew
- When a culturally specific item appears for the first time: add 【注：…】footnote in Chinese draft

## Narrative Rhythm (律动感 — Verify Before Locking Any Book Framework)

Every novel must have a full emotional arc — like physical intimacy: buildup, arousal, losing oneself, climax, slow recovery. Verify this arc is present in the chapter map before writing begins.

**The five stages and their chapter weight:**

| Stage    | 中文     | Typical weight     | What it does                                                                   |
| -------- | -------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 前戏     | Buildup  | 20–25% of chapters | Establish world, characters, stakes; reader falls in love with the protagonist |
| 动情     | Arousal  | 20–25%             | Darkness accumulates; tension rises; the reader feels the weight building      |
| 迷失     | Lost     | 25–30%             | The fall + the other world / transformation; reader is fully inside            |
| 高潮     | Climax   | 10–15%             | The peak moment(s); can be double-peaked; must be earned by everything before  |
| 缓慢恢复 | Recovery | 20–25%             | Slow rebuilding; not a happy ending but a landing                              |

**All five stages must be present and explicitly mapped.** Do not fold the climax into another stage — it must stand alone as its own chapter(s).

**Red flags in the chapter map:**

- Buildup exceeds 35% → reader loses patience before the story begins
- Darkness arc (动情) is too short → the fall feels unearned, the climax has no weight
- The climax chapter is long and explanatory → climax should be SHORT and DENSE; its power comes from accumulated pressure, not from length
- Recovery is rushed → reader doesn't get to breathe; the ending feels hollow

**Check during framework review:**
After mapping all chapters, assign each chapter to one of the five stages. If any stage is missing or severely imbalanced, restructure before writing.

## Writing Style Confirmation (文风确认 — Do This Before Writing the First Chapter of Any Book)

Before writing a single word of a new book, present Elar with 3–5 writing style options.
Do NOT begin drafting until Elar selects one. Record the confirmed style in the book's framework document.

Style options should be tailored to the book's subject, protagonist, and emotional register.
Each option should include: a one-line description, a reference author/work, and one sample sentence in the book's language.

Format:

```
**文风选项 for [Book Title]:**

A. [Style name] — [one-line description]
   参考：[Author / Work]
   示例："[one sample sentence]"

B. ...
```

After Elar selects, record in framework doc:
`- **文风**：[selected style name] — [brief description] 【已定】`

## Part Framework (框架先行 — Do This Before Writing Any Chapter)

**This is the single biggest quality lever.** Part 3 of Book 01 was written faster and with fewer revisions than Parts 1–2 because the framework was locked before writing began.

For every new Part: build and approve the framework document before writing chapter one.

Framework document lives in: `调研材料/XX-第N部框架.md`

Must contain:

1. **Part mission** — 3 bullets: what gap does this part fill? what emotional state does it leave?
2. **Character relationship tree** — for each significant relationship: dynamic + 认知弧线 (how POV's perception of this person evolves, labeled by stage) + story nodes
3. **Six mirror matrix** — each supporting character is a mirror; name the specific question each one illuminates about the protagonist
4. **Group portrait plan** — number every group scene (群像①②③) in advance; distribute across chapters; specify social positions represented
5. **Chapter map** — each chapter needs a **Function** line (what it _does_ structurally, not just what happens in it)
6. **Key writing decisions** — lock critical choices (what's in the letter, what's the final image, where the confrontation happens) before writing; tag as 【已定】or 【待讨论】; do not write until all 【待讨论】are resolved

See `references/part-framework-template.md` for full template and review checklist.
See `references/example-part3-framework.md` for a real approved framework (Book 01, Part 3) — use as a quality benchmark.

## Chapter Review Scoring (校阅评分体系)

### The Reviewer: Joanna

All chapters are reviewed by **Joanna** — a rigorous, experienced literary editor with decades of experience in fiction. She has a reputation for holding authors to the highest standards, but every book that passes her review earns exceptional reader loyalty: strong story momentum, emotional resonance, and the compulsive readability that makes readers finish in one sitting and immediately want the next book.

Joanna is not cruel, but she is honest. She will find the sentence that feels slightly off, the scene that does one thing when it should do three, the moment where the narrator sounds ten years older than she is. She signs off only when a chapter genuinely earns it.

**Minimum passing score: 90/100.** If below 90, list all deductions, revise, and re-score before submitting. Joanna does not accept "close enough."

When writing reviewer prompts, open with: _"You are Joanna, a rigorous literary editor with decades of fiction experience. You are known for high standards and for producing books with exceptional reader loyalty."_

### A. Writing Quality（写作质量）— 40 points

| Code | Criterion                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Points |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| A1   | Actions/dialogue only: no psychological analysis violations, no director-style transitions                                                                                                                                                                                                              | 10     |
| A2   | Three-layer scenes: every scene does at least 2 of 3 things (advance/reveal/cultural layer)                                                                                                                                                                                                             | 10     |
| A3   | Character consistency: speech and actions match her age, circumstances, and established voice                                                                                                                                                                                                           | 10     |
| A4   | Rhythm and restraint: no repeated sentences, no filler, seven-tenths rule applied. **Hard deduction (-10):** comma-chain run-ons (连逗句) — a single sentence with 5+ commas stringing actions/observations without a period break. Correct form: break into separate short sentences with white space. | 10     |

### B. Framework Compliance（框架符合度）— 35 points

| Code | Criterion                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Points |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| B1   | Chapter Function: fully delivers what the framework's Function line requires                                                                                                                                                                                                                | 15     |
| B2   | Mirror characters: any mirror appearing in this chapter genuinely illuminates its assigned question (not just present). For young POV narrators (age <12), sensory/atmospheric accumulation is valid — no explicit locking sentence required if the contrast is carried by concrete detail. | 10     |
| B3   | Cultural/historical accuracy: no unverified details; all uncertainties marked 【待查】                                                                                                                                                                                                      | 10     |

### C. Continuity（上下文衔接）— 25 points

| Code | Criterion                                                                                                | Points |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| C1   | Backward continuity: timeline, character states, and facts are consistent with all prior chapters        | 10     |
| C2   | Forward setup: plants the seeds/nodes required by the framework for upcoming chapters                    | 10     |
| C3   | Running lines: recurring threads (drawing line, diary line, object lines) are correctly advanced or held | 5      |

### ⚠️ Two-Round Independent Review (Self-scoring is not valid)

**Round 1 — Writing Quality Only (fast, no context needed)**
Spawn with `runTimeoutSeconds: 180`. Pass only the chapter text + A rubric.
If A-total < 35/40: return to writer immediately with deductions. Do not proceed to Round 2.
If A-total ≥ 35/40: proceed to Round 2.


## Chapter Workflow

```
1. Read adjacent chapters (before + after) for continuity
2. Run Pre-Writing Checklist (time/age/voice/research)
3. Write first draft — actions and dialogue only
4. Self-review: mark obvious A-violations (psychological analysis, transitions)
5. Revise
6. Commit draft to git
7. Spawn Round 1 reviewer (A-only, fast) → if <35/40, fix and re-run Round 1
8. Once Round 1 passes → Spawn Round 2 reviewer (B+C, with context)
9. If combined <90: fix deductions, re-commit, re-run relevant round only
10. Once 90+: notify Elar for review
```

## Three-Generation Structure (for "My Liberty" and similar)

See `references/my-liberty-structure.md` for the full character/timeline reference for Book 01.

For future multi-generation books, apply the same倒流 (reverse-flow) structure:

- Open in present (youngest generation, abroad or displaced)
- Flow backward through mother's generation, then grandmother's
- Return to present with accumulated weight
- Epilogue: youngest generation performs an act that echoes across all three generations

## Joanna Review Tracking

Every book must maintain a `JOANNA_REVIEW_LOG.md` in the book's root folder.

**Format:**

- One table per review round (Round 1, Round 2, translation review, etc.)
- Columns: Issue | Chapter | Status (✅ Fixed / ⚠️ Pending / 🚫 Won't fix + reason)
- Append, never overwrite — the log is a permanent record

**Rules:**

- After every Joanna review session, all issues must be entered into the log before the next chapter is written or translated
- Before starting a new session, read the log to check for pending items
- "Pending" items must be resolved or explicitly marked "Won't fix" before the book is published
- Translation error log lives in `english/TRANSLATION_NOTES.md` under "Translation Error Log" section — append errors after each chapter review

## Git Discipline

- Commit after every substantive change (never batch multiple chapters in one commit)
- Commit message format: `book-XX: 章节名——核心改动描述`
- Push immediately after commit
- Never leave uncommitted changes overnight
