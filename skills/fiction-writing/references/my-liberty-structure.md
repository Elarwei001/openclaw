## Three Characters

### Maryam (祖母)

- Born: 1938, Tehran
- Profession: Primary school Persian language teacher
- Key relationships: Arash (lost love, executed 1981), unnamed husband (government clerk), daughter Shireen
- Voice: Spare, observational. Never explains herself. Love expressed through objects and silence.
- Arc: Self-possession under compression — wears head scarf but keeps the blue dress; votes in secret; writes a letter she never sends

### Shireen (母亲)

- Born: 1963, Tehran
- Profession: Factory accountant
- Key relationships: Dariush (husband, government clerk), daughter Neda
- Voice: Quieter than her younger self. Watches more than she speaks. Answers questions with questions.
- Arc: Revolution → disillusionment → endurance. Raised Neda to leave, even though she stayed.

### Neda (女儿)

- Born: **1986**, Tehran
- Profession: Frontend developer (private software SME, ~10 people)
- Education: Computer Science, Tehran University, 2004–2008
- Key relationships: Kamran (unnamed in text, 1984, married, disappeared Sept 2022), Reza (university classmate, mirror figure), Sareh (Toronto friend, Instagram)
- Voice: Direct, dry. Holds distance from sentiment. Her silences are different from her mother's — hers are chosen, not imposed.
- Arc: Legal departure via work visa (2023) → Berlin → receives box → reads grandmother's letter → cooks

## Key Objects (传承线)

- **Blue dress**: Maryam buys it after voting (self-possession); stored 1980; sent to Neda in box
- **Forough collection**: Arash gives to Maryam; 1977 letter stored inside; reaches Neda via box
- **Khoresh Ghormeh Sabzi recipe**: Maryam → Shireen → Neda. Neda cooks it in Berlin; taste is wrong.
- **Deep-red cloth**: Wedding gift → Shireen makes it into Neda's childhood coat; Neda keeps it

## Iron Rules for This Book

1. Forough appears **only in Ch.6 (箱子)** — never before as a named encounter for Neda
2. Neda left legally via work visa — never use "exile" framing
3. Kamran is **never named** in the narrative text (only in planning docs)
4. One internal monologue exception: "原来是这样啊" in Part 1 Ch.2 (wedding night) — sealed, do not repeat
5. Dariush's name never given to the husband in Part 1 — he is always "她丈夫"
6. No complete romance arc for Neda — Kamran has 4 scenes max
